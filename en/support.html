HERE TO HELP

Strongary support

Found a bug or have an idea for an improvement? Get in touch — I will help you work it out.

Contact emailstrongary.app@gmail.com ↗

What helps me understand the issue

  • Your iPhone model and iOS version. For Watch issues, include the Apple Watch model and watchOS version.
  • Your Strongary version: Settings → About.
  • What you were doing, what you expected and what actually happened.
  • A screenshot, if it helps explain the issue.

Where your data is stored

Your records are stored on your devices and, when sync is enabled, in your personal iCloud. The developer cannot access these records. If you send an email or a screenshot, I can only see what you choose to include.

Data obtained from Apple Health is not included in Strongary’s iCloud sync. Read more in the privacy policy.
