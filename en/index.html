BEFORE YOUR FIRST WORKOUT

A few more
answers.

What kinds of workouts is Strongary for?

For strength training and cardio. Log exercises, sets, weight and reps, or track time and distance for cardio. You choose your own program and training load.

Do I need an Apple Watch?

No. The diary, templates, charts and supplement tracking are available on iPhone. Apple Watch adds wrist-based workout controls and heart rate recording during your session.

What does supplement tracking include?

Schedules for supplements and vitamins, reminders, intake logging and consistency statistics. Strongary does not track food calories or macros.

When and on which devices can I try it?

We’re preparing a public beta through TestFlight. The invitation will appear on this page when it opens. You’ll need an iPhone with iOS 18.6 or later; the watch app requires watchOS 11 or later.
