Installation

Strongary runs on iPhone with iOS 18.6 or later. Apple Watch requires watchOS 11 or later and a compatible iPhone. There is no Android version.

Install the iPhone app from the App Store. Regional availability and device requirements are listed on its product page.

Open in the App Store ↗

Apple Watch

To install on your watch, open the Watch app on iPhone. Find Strongary in the list of available apps and tap Install.

If your devices show different records

  1. Open Strongary on both iPhone and Apple Watch.
  2. Check that your watch is connected to your iPhone and both devices have the latest app version.
  3. If the issue persists, tell support which device you started the workout on and which action did not sync.

iCloud and backups

In Strongary, open Settings → iCloud & Backups. You can check sync status, create a backup and view saved backups here.

Syncing requires iCloud to be available for your Apple Account. Sync is enabled by default; the diary still works on your device without it.

If records are missing or not updating, first save a backup file and contact support. Avoid deleting the app or restoring an older backup before checking: this may replace records you need.

To keep a separate backup, select it under Backups on This iPhone → Share and save the file. Backups inside the app are deleted with it; iCloud also syncs deletions and does not replace a separate backup file.

Apple Health

In Strongary, open Settings → Apple Health to check access. Manage permissions for individual data types in Health: profile → apps → Strongary. Menu names may vary by iOS version.

If heart rate or watch workout results are missing, check the relevant permissions on iPhone and Apple Watch. You can still keep a manual diary without Health access.

Read about the data the app uses in the privacy policy.

Reminders

Check the days and times in your workout schedule or the relevant supplement. Then allow notifications for Strongary in iPhone settings.

Focus modes, silent mode and Apple Watch notification settings can affect where and how reminders appear.

What to include

  1. What you were trying to do, what happened and whether it happens again.
  2. Your Strongary version from Settings → About.
  3. Your iPhone model and iOS version. For watch issues, include the Apple Watch model and watchOS version.

Only attach a screenshot if it helps, and hide personal details. Do not send passwords, sign-in codes, medical documents or a full backup.

The developer cannot see records on your devices or in your private iCloud database. Support helps with the app; it does not provide training programs or supplement prescriptions.
