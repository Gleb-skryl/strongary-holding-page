Data in the app

Strongary stores workouts, exercises, sets, templates, schedules, supplement and vitamin intakes, your profile, weight and settings on your devices. This data supports your journal, planning and progress calculations. No separate Strongary account is required.

The app has no advertising or its own analytics trackers. The developer has no server holding your journal and cannot access your private iCloud database. Technical logs remain on your devices and are not automatically sent to the developer.

Data is protected by Apple’s security features. Apple may provide the developer with App Store statistics and diagnostics according to its policies and your device’s data-sharing settings.

iCloud sync

Sync is enabled by default when iCloud is available. Records are stored in your Apple Account’s private database. Manage sync in Strongary: Settings → iCloud & Backups.

Journal entries and manually entered data sync, including your profile and weight. Data from Health and Apple Watch sensors is excluded from Strongary’s sync.

Turning off sync stops updates between devices. Records already stored in iCloud remain there. The service follows Apple’s privacy policy.

Health, Apple Watch and permissions

With your permission, Strongary reads weight, height, date of birth, resting heart rate, workouts, heart rate and active energy from Health. This data supports weight history, workout metrics and heart rate zones. Your entered weight, Apple Watch workouts and their active energy may be saved to Health. The watch exchanges records with its paired iPhone through Apple’s services.

Health data is not sold, used for advertising or automatically sent to the developer. You can revoke permissions in Health. This stops access but does not delete previously saved records. Apple manages Health’s own sync.

Workout and supplement reminders are created on your device with your permission. You can turn them off in system notification settings.

Backups

Strongary stores backups on your device. Automatic backups are enabled by default and created while you use the app, no more than once every three days. The 10 most recent files are kept, counting automatic backups and pre-restore snapshots together. Older files are deleted automatically. Manual backups remain until you delete them.

A backup contains your journal and health metrics, including heart rate and calories. In iCloud & Backups, you can save it as a separate file or share it with someone. You choose where to store the file and who can access its contents.

iPhone backup is a separate Apple feature and may include Strongary data. Its settings are independent of the sync switch inside the app.

Storage and deletion

Records remain until you delete them. With sync enabled, changes and deletions are shared with linked devices through iCloud.

  • On your device, delete records and backups in Strongary. Deleting the app removes its local data; offloading the app keeps it.
  • Delete iCloud data through system iCloud storage settings. First turn off sync on linked devices. Deleting the app does not clear iCloud.
  • Records in Health and files saved elsewhere must be deleted separately. Deleting Strongary does not affect them.

The developer cannot remotely view or delete records on your devices or in your private iCloud.

Website

The website does not receive your journal from the app. It has no analytics counters, advertising trackers or data collection forms.

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. This service logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. Read more in the GitHub privacy statement.

Support requests

When you contact support, the developer receives your email address, provided name, message and attachments. They are used to reply and help with your question, not for marketing emails.

A description of the issue and the app version are enough for your first message. Please do not send health data or a full journal backup.

Correspondence is kept while it is needed to resolve your question, including any related disagreements. Any required retention period is also taken into account. Messages are then deleted or anonymized.

Support email uses Gmail under the Google privacy policy. Google and GitHub may process information outside your country.

Questions about your data

The developer responsible for processing support requests is Gleb Skryl, an individual based in Russia. You can write to strongary.app@gmail.com.

Use this address to ask what information the developer holds about you, or to request corrections or deletion. Depending on the rules that apply, you can also request your data for transfer, restrict its use, object to processing or withdraw consent for future processing.

Verification of your email address may be needed to protect your data. Requests are handled within legal deadlines. You can also contact Roskomnadzor or another data protection authority.

If data processing changes, an updated policy will appear here with a new date.
