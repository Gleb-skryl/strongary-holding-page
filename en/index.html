ABOUT THE APP

Your questions, answered.

What kinds of workouts is Strongary for?

For strength training: exercises, sets, weight and reps. For cardio: time and distance. You choose your own program and training load.

Do I need an Apple Watch?

No. The diary, templates, charts and supplement tracking are available on iPhone. Apple Watch adds wrist-based workout controls and heart rate recording during your session.

Can I track food calories and macros?

No. Strongary tracks supplements and vitamins: their schedules, reminders and intake history. It does not include a food diary.

How do I install the beta?

You’ll need TestFlight, Apple’s app for installing beta versions. The Strongary link will appear at the bottom of this page. You’ll need an iPhone with iOS 18.6 or later; the watch app requires watchOS 11 or later.
