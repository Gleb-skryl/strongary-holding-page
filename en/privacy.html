YOUR DATA

Privacy

Updated October 17, 2026

The app and the website serve different purposes and handle data differently. This page explains what Strongary stores, how iCloud works and why the website uses visitor statistics.

The Strongary app

Strongary stores your workouts, exercises, sets, templates, schedules, intakes, profile and settings on your devices. No separate Strongary account is needed. The app has no developer-operated server for your records, advertising trackers or built-in usage analytics.

iCloud sync

When sync is enabled, records are also saved to the private iCloud database of your Apple Account. This lets you sync your data and restore it on another iPhone. Record contents are encrypted using Apple’s services; the developer cannot access them.

Sync includes workouts without Apple Health metrics, templates, schedules, intakes, custom exercises, manually entered profile data and some settings. Strongary does not send data from Health or device sensors, including heart rate and calories, to this iCloud database.

You can manage sync in Settings → Data. Without iCloud, the app continues to work with local records. Storage in iCloud is also governed by Apple’s privacy policy.

Apple Health and Apple Watch

With your permission, Strongary reads workout and profile data from Health, including heart rate, active energy, weight and height, and writes completed workouts and weight you enter. You can manage these permissions in the Health app.

This data is not used for advertising or sent to website analytics. Apple Health manages its own cross-device sync separately from Strongary’s iCloud sync. The Apple Watch app exchanges data with your iPhone through Apple’s system services.

Backups and deletion

You can save a backup as a separate file. If you export it to iCloud Drive or another service, storage then depends on the service you choose.

Deleting the app removes its local data, but does not itself delete records from iCloud or Apple Health. You can manage iCloud data in your Apple Account settings and Health records in the Health app. With sync enabled, record changes and deletions in Strongary are synced to iCloud and your linked devices.

Website visitor statistics

Strongary.com uses Yandex Metrica to understand how many people visit and which pages they view. These are website statistics, not data about your workouts or intakes in the app.

The service uses cookies and processes visit information, including IP address, browser, device, pages viewed and referral source. Session Replay and click maps are disabled. You can restrict cookies in your browser settings. For details, see the Yandex privacy policy and Yandex Metrica terms.

Your chosen site theme is saved in your browser. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages; its operation is also covered by the GitHub privacy statement.

Email and feedback

The website has no email collection, voting or feedback forms. If you contact support, the developer receives your email address, message and any attachments so they can respond. Email is handled by Gmail.

Feedback previously submitted on the website is not published. For data questions, removal of an earlier response or support, email strongary.app@gmail.com.

Policy changes

If data handling changes, this page and its update date will be revised.
