Install Strongary

Strongary is a free app for iPhone and Apple Watch. It requires iOS 18.6 or later on iPhone and watchOS 11 or later on Apple Watch. Apple Watch is optional: your journal, plans, charts and supplement tracking are available on iPhone.

Install Strongary from the App Store. To install it on your watch, open the Watch app on iPhone, find Strongary among the available apps and tap Install.

View on the App Store ↗

Workouts on Apple Watch

Open Strongary on your iPhone and watch. You can start a workout, log sets, track rest and review your workout summary on the watch. Supplement check-ins are also available.

If records are not updating, check the connection between your watch and iPhone and open Strongary on both devices. If the issue persists, contact support and describe your last action.

iCloud and backups

Open Settings → iCloud and backups in Strongary to find sync status and backups. Sync is enabled by default and requires access to your Apple Account’s iCloud.

Before restoring or moving your records, export an up-to-date backup file. Local backups are stored inside the app and are deleted with it. If records are missing or fail to sync, save a backup first and contact support.

Health permissions and notifications

Manage permissions in Health: profile → apps → Strongary. Allow the relevant data types to see heart rate and active energy during workouts. Manage reminders in system notification settings.

What to include in your message

  • Your iPhone model and iOS version; for watch issues, your Apple Watch model and watchOS version.
  • Your Strongary version from Settings → About.
  • The steps before the issue, what you expected and what happened.
  • A screenshot if it helps. Hide personal information that is not needed to understand the issue.

The developer cannot see your records on your device or in iCloud. Support only receives information you choose to send.

For deleting records, managing backups and revoking permissions, see the privacy policy.
