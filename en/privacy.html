Data in the app

Strongary stores workouts, exercises, sets, templates, schedules, supplement and vitamin intakes, your profile, weight and settings on your devices. This data supports your journal, planning, suggestions and progress calculations. No separate Strongary account is required.

The app has no advertising or its own analytics trackers. The developer has no server holding your journal and cannot access your private iCloud database. Technical logs remain on your devices and are not automatically sent to the developer.

Records are protected using Apple’s system features. Apple may also provide the developer with App Store statistics and diagnostics under its policies and your device’s data-sharing settings.

iCloud sync

Sync is enabled by default when iCloud is available. Records are stored in your Apple Account’s private database. Manage sync in Strongary: Settings → iCloud & Backups.

Journal entries sync, including your manually entered profile and weight. Data from Health and Apple Watch sensors is excluded from Strongary’s sync; manually entered heart rate and calories in older workouts may be stored in iCloud.

Turning sync off stops data exchange but does not delete records already stored. The service operates under the Apple privacy policy.

Health, Apple Watch and permissions

With your permission, Strongary reads weight, height, date of birth, resting heart rate, workouts, heart rate and active energy for weight history, workout metrics and heart rate zones. Your entered weight, Apple Watch workouts and their active energy may be saved to Health. The watch exchanges records with its paired iPhone through Apple’s services.

Health data is not sold, used for advertising or automatically sent to the developer. You can revoke permissions in the Health app; this stops access but does not delete previously saved records. Apple manages Health’s own sync.

Workout and intake reminders are scheduled on your device with your permission. You can turn them off in system notification settings.

Backups

Strongary creates local data backups. Automatic backups are on by default: a new one is created while using the app once at least three days have passed since the last. Automatic backups and snapshots made before restoring share one history of the 10 most recent files. Older files are removed automatically. Manually created backups remain until you delete them.

A local backup includes your journal and health metrics, including heart rate and calories. Manage and export backups in iCloud & Backups. When exporting, you choose where to save the file or whom to send it to; the recipient receives its contents.

iPhone Backup is a separate Apple feature that may include Strongary data. Excluding Strongary from iPhone Backup does not turn off in-app sync, and vice versa. These settings are independent.

Storage and deletion

Records remain until you delete them. With sync enabled, changes and deletions are shared between linked devices through iCloud.

  • On your device, delete entries and backups in Strongary. Deleting the app removes local data; offloading keeps it.
  • In iCloud, manage Strongary data in system iCloud storage settings. Turn off sync on linked devices before deleting cloud data. Deleting the app does not clear iCloud.
  • In Health and exported files, delete data separately. Deleting Strongary does not remove it.

The developer cannot remotely view or delete records on your devices or in your private iCloud.

Website

The website does not receive your journal from the app. It has no analytics counters, advertising trackers or data collection forms.

GitHub Pages hosting logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. Processing is described in the GitHub privacy policy.

Support requests

When you contact support, the developer receives your email address, provided name, message and attachments. They are used to answer you and investigate the issue, not for marketing. Do not send health data or a full journal backup: a description of the issue and the app version are enough for your first message.

The legal basis is performing obligations to support the app; for a specific dispute, a legitimate interest in protecting rights; and, where applicable, legal requirements. Without a reply address and a description of the issue, it may not be possible to respond.

Correspondence is kept until the issue is resolved and, where necessary, until a related dispute or mandatory retention period ends. It is then deleted or anonymized.

Support email uses Gmail under the Google privacy policy. Google and GitHub may process information outside your country; linking to their policies does not constitute consent to additional processing purposes.
