Privacy policy

Updated November 6, 2026

How Strongary handles records on iPhone and Apple Watch, what syncs and how to manage your data.

Strongary is developed by Gleb Skryl, an individual based in the Russian Federation. The developer is responsible for handling support requests. Send privacy questions and requests to the email below.

strongary.app@gmail.com

Your records in the app

Strongary stores workouts, exercises, sets, templates, schedules, supplement and vitamin intakes, profile information and settings on your devices. These records power your journal, planning, suggestions and progress calculations. No registration or separate Strongary account is required.

The app has no advertising, usage analytics or advertising trackers. The developer does not operate a server containing your records and cannot access the contents of your private iCloud database. Support only receives records if you choose to send them in an email or attachment.

The app keeps a size-limited technical log on the device to diagnose errors. A watch connectivity log may be transferred to the paired iPhone. It is not automatically sent to the developer.

iCloud sync

Sync is enabled by default and works when your iCloud account is available on the device. Records are stored in your Apple Account’s private CloudKit database, with record contents encrypted using Apple’s services. Manage sync in Strongary under Settings → iCloud and backups.

Sync includes workouts, templates, schedules, custom exercises, supplements and intakes, manually entered profile and body weight information, and some settings. Strongary excludes data obtained from Apple Health and Apple Watch sensors from its own iCloud sync. This exclusion does not apply to information you enter manually.

Turning sync off stops data exchange but does not delete records already stored in iCloud. You can continue using your journal locally without iCloud.

Apple’s services are also covered by its privacy policy.

Apple Health and Apple Watch

You grant access separately for each data type. Strongary may read body weight, height, date of birth, resting heart rate, workouts, heart rate and active energy. These are used for weight history, workout statistics, heart rate zones and retrieving workout results from Apple Watch.

With permission, Strongary saves the body weight you enter and workouts recorded with Apple Watch to Apple Health. The watch app exchanges records with the paired iPhone through Apple’s system services.

Strongary does not use Health data for advertising, sell it or automatically send it to the developer or website analytics. If you export a backup or send a screenshot yourself, the selected data will reach the recipient. Apple manages Health’s own sync separately from Strongary’s sync.

You can change or revoke permissions in the Health app: profile → apps → Strongary. Revoking access stops further reading or writing of that data type, but does not itself delete previously saved records.

Reminders

Workout and supplement reminders are scheduled on the device with your permission. You can turn them off in the notification settings on your iPhone or Apple Watch.

Backups

Automatic backups are enabled by default and are created while using the app at intervals of at least three days. They are stored in the app’s local folder. The latest 10 automatic backups and pre-restore snapshots are retained together; manual backups remain until deleted.

Backups may contain workout history and health metrics, including heart rate and calories. Use iCloud and backups to manage backups and export a file. If you save it to iCloud Drive, send it to support or another service, a copy will be stored in that destination under that service’s storage practices.

Retention and deletion

Journal records remain until you delete them. With sync enabled, changes and deletions are propagated to iCloud and connected devices. Turning iCloud sync off does not erase data.

  • On your device: delete records in Strongary. Deleting the app, rather than offloading it, removes its local data and backups.
  • In iCloud: turn off Strongary sync on connected devices and manage the app’s data in system settings under Apple Account → iCloud → storage management. Deleting the app does not delete cloud records.
  • In Health: delete records separately in the Health app. Deleting Strongary does not automatically erase them.
  • Exported files: delete them from Files, email or the service where you saved them.

The developer cannot remotely view or delete records on your devices or in your private iCloud. Contact support if you need help managing your data.

The strongary.com website

The website does not receive records from the app. It has no advertising trackers, email collection forms or active analytics counters. Your selected appearance is saved locally in the browser and can be removed by clearing site data.

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit, the host processes technical information, including your IP address, to serve and protect the website. See the GitHub privacy statement.

The site used Yandex Metrica before November 5, 2026. The counter has been disabled; this does not automatically delete information previously processed by the service. That processing is described in the Yandex privacy policy.

Support emails

If you email support, the developer receives your sender address, the name you provide, your message and any attachments. These are used to respond and investigate the issue. Do not send health data or a full backup: an issue description and app version are enough for your first message.

Email is handled through Gmail and is also covered by the Google privacy policy.

Correspondence is retained for as long as needed to handle your request, resolve the reported issue or address a related dispute. Any longer retention period required by law also applies. Once no longer needed, data is to be deleted or anonymized; the mailbox does not have an automatic deletion period. This also applies to responses submitted through the site’s former feedback form.

Purposes and legal grounds

Journal records are processed on your devices to provide the features you choose. System permissions control Health access and notifications. They do not give the developer access to your records or replace separate consent where required by law.

Necessary information in a support request is processed to support your use of the app and respond to you. The legal basis is performing obligations related to your use of the app; for a specific dispute, it is the legitimate interest in protecting legal rights, to the extent permitted by law. Information may also be retained to comply with legal obligations.

Contacting support is voluntary. Without a reply address and issue description, the developer may be unable to respond. Messages are not used for advertising emails or advertising profiles.

External services

Apple provides iCloud, Health and device connectivity; GitHub hosts the website; Google handles support email. These services process information under their own policies, linked above. GitHub and Google state that they process data in multiple countries, including outside the user’s country.

Opening an external link or sending an email involves the relevant service. This policy describes current processing and is not consent to additional purposes not described here.

Your rights and requests

To access, correct or delete information received by the developer, email strongary.app@gmail.com. Where applicable, you may also request restriction or portability, object to processing based on legitimate interests, and withdraw consent previously given. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of earlier processing.

Include the address you used to contact support and identify the message or information concerned. To protect your data, confirmation that you control that address may be needed. Requests are handled within the time limits set by applicable law. You may contact Roskomnadzor or the competent data protection authority where you live.

These requests concern information held by the developer. You manage records on your device, in your private iCloud and in Health; deletion steps are explained above.

Policy changes

If our data practices change, an updated version will be posted here with a new revision date.
